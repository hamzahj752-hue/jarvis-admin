SMS & Call Assistant Policy

What the assistants are permitted to do, stored in public.jarvis_assistant_policy and served to every device by the jarvis-languages endpoint. Writes are restricted by row-level security to an authenticated account whose trusted app_metadata.role is admin. Until this row is written these features run on their shipped defaults, not on nothing - an admin switch here is what makes the matching client behaviour real.

SMS assistant

SMS assistant enabled
The master switch for message announcements and replies. Off means the assistant stays out of notifications entirely.
Announce incoming messages
Say who the message is from, without drafting a reply.
Draft replies
Offer a draft and open the compose sheet. The user still approves the exact words, and nothing is ever sent from this app.
Attribution text
Appended to each draft so the recipient can tell it was dictated. Max 120 characters. Shown to a third party, so it is bounded in the database too.
confirmation_required and attribution_enabled are not shown as switches because they are not negotiable: the app ignores an explicit false for both, so a reply cannot be composed or sent without the user approving it and every reply stays attributed.
Enforced on device

Call assistant

Call assistant enabled
The master switch for reacting to incoming calls. Off means JARVIS ignores the phone entirely.
Announce incoming calls
Passive: say who is calling, then wait. Turning this off also stops the assistant offering an answer, because there would be nothing to answer about.
Offer to answer
Allow “should I answer?” and act on the user’s word. The device also needs the Answer Phone Calls permission; where that is missing the app announces without asking, rather than offering something it cannot do.
Answer automatically
The most dangerous switch on this page. On, JARVIS connects a ringing call with nobody asked. Only ever turn this on for a device you control. Off unless deliberately enabled.
Reject automatically
End an unanswered ringing call. This also needs the default-dialer role, which this app does not request; without it the switch has no effect and the app says so instead of claiming the call was rejected.
confirmation_required_before_answering is not a switch for the same reason: the user’s word is always required before a call is connected.
Enforced on device

Timeouts

Draft body timeout (ms)
How long “what should I say?” stays open. 5–120000.
Draft confirmation timeout (ms)
How long the exact draft stays approvable. 5–120000.
Call decision timeout (ms)
How long “should I answer?” stays open. 5–60000.
